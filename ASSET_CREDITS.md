## User-supplied USEK imagery

The app keeps the following user-supplied images in `frontend/public/images/`. Duplicate source copies and unused design references have been removed.

| File | Used by |
|---|---|
| `usek-logo.webp` | Site header and footer |
| `usek-campus.webp` | Add/edit item form sidebar |

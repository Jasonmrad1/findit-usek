# Asset credits

The eleven SVG images in `frontend/public/images/` are original vector illustrations created for FindIt USEK. They are illustrative stand-ins, **not photographs** and **not depictions of actual USEK campus facilities or real lost-and-found reports**. They contain no external stock images, brand logos, personal documents, or identifiable people.

## Showcase report photography

The 14 fictional reports now favor everyday item-in-context photographs: belongings on benches, classroom desks, study tables, and ordinary seating areas. Catalog product shots and dramatic studio backgrounds have been replaced. These remain sourced photographs, not authenticated photos of lost property at USEK.

Item and indoor location photos are sourced from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Outdoor location context uses the user's supplied USEK courtyard photograph. It illustrates the campus environment, not the precise bench where an item was reported. The separate item and location photos do not depict the same verified incident.

External photo hosts are blocked in this development environment, so photographs use fixed online image URLs. Selection was based on source-page descriptions; the rendered remote photographs could not be visually verified here. No AI-generated photographs or artificial distress effects were used.
